Dropped out midway through the third year having realised tech was where I belonged

Obsessed with coding

By the time I reached the second year of this course I had become completely fixated on learning to code. The bookshelves in my student flat should have been filled with textbooks on Latin American anthropology and political science, but instead mine were full of things like K&R, the Camel Book, and Mythical Man Month.

I knew by now that coding was what I wanted to do for a job, but I had made an unconscious assumption that everyone who had a job like that must be a genius.

My four month long year abroad

The third year of this course was the dissertation year, spent abroad researching and writing a very long essay about society, culture and politics in Latin America. My chosen topic was “Digital Inclusion”, and my goal was to study the impact of things like Linux and the One Laptop Per Child initiative on access to computing.

Instead I spent four months in Rio de Janeiro building a Pong! clone in C, attending a C programming module at PUC-Rio, and playing pickup basketball in Jardim de Alah. Back in England for Christmas, I formally withdrew from the course and applied at the Department of Computer Science.
