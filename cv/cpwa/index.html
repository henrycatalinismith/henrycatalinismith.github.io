Studied like hell for weeks and passed both the CPACC exam and the (very difficult) WAS exam to achieve the IAAP’s highest available credential.

Studying

Due to Soundtrap’s strategic focus on schools there was a sufficiently strong business case for certification that I was able to get the whole shebang expensed. This was several thousands of dollars, which meant it would have been very embarrassing to fail the exams.

So I took the studying pretty seriously. This entailed a couple of notebooks, a minor doomsday stockpile of flash cards, and a good hour or two per night over a period of many weeks. No small feat with two toddlers charging around.

Taking the exams

This was my first time taking a serious exam online rather than in person. I was curious how secure the process would seem, and what I experienced was reassuring.

The WAS exam was by far the hardest of the two. It’s very technical and very deep. Not something you can chance your way through after a few minutes on the Wikipedia page for JAWS.
