Wargames

Multiplayer global thermonuclear war

Wargames was a viral game I made back in 2014. It used the Google Maps API and a Node.js backend to implement multiplayer thermonuclear war in the form of crudely animated geodesic lines. It went unexpectedly viral due to news coverage of an incident where my whiteboard designs for the game were reported to the police by a suspicious landlord.

Adrenaline-Fuelled Performance Tuning

Due to the surge of traffic from the news coverage, several hundred people were typically online playing the game simultaneously for a number of weeks after launch. The Google Maps JS API (notably not a game engine) couldn’t cope, and the framerate nosedived.

I tried just abot everything I could think of, in a series of slightly panicked performance tuning sessions. Capping the number of missiles rendered was both the most impactful and simplest optimisation that came out of those efforts.

WebGL Playback

The game itself is no longer online. Usage dropped to zero and over the years it became an unrewarding burden to keep the server running, so I shut it down. As the game had been an important milestone in my career, I disliked having nothing online to show for it.

So sometimes during the pandemic I dug out some of the old missile launch logs from the game’s heyday, and used WebGL to build a 3D visualisation of a random sample period of gameplay. That’s what’s online today.
