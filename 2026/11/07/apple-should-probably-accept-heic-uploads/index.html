Apple should probably accept `.HEIC` uploads

2026-11-07

  1. Henry Catalini Smith
  2. └── ❯ Blog
  3. └── ❯ Apple should probably accept `.HEIC` uploads

You ever take a photo of a document for some official process and then had to convert from .HEIC to something that websites will actually accept before you could upload it? I sure have. Understandable when it’s a website run by normies but today I was sorting out my official documentation for an App Store release and had the same problem.

I dunno I thought that was quite damning, really. HEIC has had 9 years to win the world over and it hasn’t even won the whole of Apple over yet.
